2026-11-18Official demo / 1

Genie 4: edit a world live in the browser

Google DeepMind opened Genie 4 to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers: change weather, add characters or move the camera in a running world with natural language; native audio for the first time; a Project Genie agent builds worlds through conversation, and a community gallery publishes and remixes them. Official page linked; no video copied.

Why it matters

It shows a model output that is not a video but a world you can keep editing: mid-session prompting turns generation from a one-shot request into continuous interaction, while native audio changes with the scene. For games, spatial design, interactive learning and robotics simulation, it is 2026’s closest public demo of a playable world model.

Boundary / what it does not claim

Currently limited to eligible AI Ultra subscribers, with Pro on a waitlist; no parameter count, benchmark, context or API price is disclosed, and developers cannot call it directly. Google lists latency, physics fidelity, audio precision and scene persistence as open problems; all output carries SynthID. It does not claim production-grade simulation or replacement of the traditional 3D pipeline.

Sources：Google official blog (2026-11-18) · DeepMind Genie 4 model page
