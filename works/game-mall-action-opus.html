How to play: Start from the title screen; all three branches share the same control goals, with differences in implementation and feel. Boundary: A multi-model branch of one brief, not a controlled score; all three are indexed separately for side-by-side viewing; this site only checked that the author page opens and captured a first-frame thumbnail, not a full play-through, and does not treat HTTP 200 as a quality verdict.
