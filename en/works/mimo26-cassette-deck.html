Aurora TD-770: A Deck That Actually Transports

Model: MiMo V2.6 Pro. Original publisher page, source, prompt and licence are below.

Demo date 2026-11-20

Open original ↗ Compare →

About this piece

A 1980s-style fictional deck: a wood-dark room, brushed-metal chassis with four corner screws, and a deep tape window holding two conic-gradient reels, a tape line and a slightly crooked cream handwritten label. Below are a four-digit counter, sixteen-segment two-channel VU strips and six mechanical keys that sink when pressed. Play transports, pause freezes, stop resets, Prev/Fwd jump tracks, Eject flips the side and cross-fades the label, all advanced by one 250 ms interval. The craft is animation-play-state transport and LED peak-hold. Boundary: no real audio; the VU levels are generated.
