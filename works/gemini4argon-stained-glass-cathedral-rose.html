这件作品About this piece

程序化径向 SVG 玫瑰窗：厚重的黑色铅条、尖拱花瓣、三叶饰和中心圆窗，背后是哥特拱顶的暗色中殿；花瓣对称度 8–24 可调，晚祷光照透明度可调，三套中世纪玻璃配色（沙特尔钴蓝/鲁比、圣礼拜堂紫/玫瑰、兰斯灰彩/祖母绿）。看点是旋转复制和铅条描边的节奏。边界：装饰性几何，不是历史窗样的精确复刻。A procedural radial SVG rose window: heavy black lead cames, lancet petals, trefoils and a central oculus inside a dark vaulted nave; petal symmetry runs 8–24, vespers luminance is adjustable, with three medieval palettes (Chartres cobalt/ruby, Sainte-Chapelle violet/rose, Reims grisaille/emerald). The craft is rotational instancing and leaded-stroke rhythm. Boundary: decorative geometry, not an exact historical reconstruction.
